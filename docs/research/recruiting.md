# Recording volunteers and children

Nothing here can happen before Nov 20. For the final, we use the data we have.

## What blocks it

Every path to new recordings needs two things first:

1. **An approved IRB study**, submitted through Cayuse to UTD's Office of Human Subjects Protections.
2. **A faculty Principal Investigator.** For the Callier registry it must be Callier-adjacent
   faculty in BBS (School of Behavioral and Brain Sciences). A student cannot lead.

Finding that faculty lead is the single biggest open step. Asked the IRB office on Nov 13 whether
recording our own three voices needs review, and what adult volunteers would need.

## The Callier Clinical Research Registry

A database of people who agreed to be contacted for studies. Free; a matching report takes about
two weeks.

1. Add the registry to the IRB as a recruitment avenue (no more explanation needed).
2. Email the registry to start.
3. The coordinator shares a Box folder with the PI; upload the IRB approval and keep it current.
4. Send the criteria form: age range, race or gender needs, English-only or not, and descriptors.
   Useful descriptors for us: no concerns (typical speech), Articulation/Phonological disorder,
   Childhood Apraxia of Speech, Cleft palate, Hearing loss, Cochlear implant user.
5. A spreadsheet of matching people appears in the Box folder.
6. We contact them. The first message must say: "You are being contacted for this study because
   you agreed to be included in the Callier participant registry." Then consent as usual.
7. People can always say no.
8. Put the list of enrolled people in the Box folder.
9. Repeat per study; report anyone who asks to be removed.

For children, we contact the parents under our own IRB.

## What we learned about reaching out

- Numbered, specific questions get real answers within hours. Vague asks ("any pointer would help")
  get "what do you want?".
- Check every address on a current official page: faculty move, and old pages stay up.
- Clinicians who replied could not meet before Nov 20; these are relationships for after the final.
