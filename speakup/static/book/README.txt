Figures for the Sound Book: schematic drawings made for Speak Up from the cited tables (figs.py and the guide figure
scripts in speakup-ui-lab). Not recordings, no outside images, no child data. Rebuild them there and copy here.

synth/: computer-made spectrograms for the book's picture viewer, synthesized from published averages with
speakup/synth.py (a Klatt-style formant synthesizer) and drawn by scripts/build_synth_pictures.py, which also checks
every one against speakup/acoustics.analyse. index.json lists each picture's sound, speaker group, values (measured or
estimated, with sources) and check result. No recordings and no audio files: the Play sound is made on request.
